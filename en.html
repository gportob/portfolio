#About me

I hold degrees in Software Engineering and Advertising, and I work as a full-stack developer and graphic designer. That combination lets me bring technical, strategic and visual thinking to every project.

I specialize in web accessibility (WCAG), technical SEO, AEO, GEO and AIO, performance and usability — because a beautiful interface is worthless if people can't use it or find it.

On the code side, I build custom institutional websites, e-commerce stores and web platforms. On the design side, I create ad campaigns, brand identities and brand guidelines. I'm open to remote, on-site and hybrid opportunities.
